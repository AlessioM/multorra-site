Multorra — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-16

Multorra ("the game") is developed by Classic Forest Games.

Data collection and sharing

The game does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal or sensitive user data from anyone, including children. It contains no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, user accounts, or developer-operated online service, and it does not request network access.

Your progress (levels, stars, coins) and settings are stored only on your device. The game does not send them to the developer or to a third party.

Browser version

The game can also be played in a web browser from the Multorra website. There, your progress and settings are kept in your browser's local storage on your device and are not sent to the developer. The website and game files are hosted by GitHub Pages; when your browser downloads them, GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address as described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement. The developer does not receive these logs and the website uses no cookies, analytics or advertising.

Retention and deletion

Local game data remains on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall the game. In the browser version, it remains until you clear this site's data in your browser. Because the developer never receives this data, there is no server-side copy to retrieve or delete.

Security

The game does not transmit user data over a network. Protect access to your device as usual.

Children

Multorra is a math game that children may play. Because it collects no personal data at all, no personal data from children is collected, used or disclosed.

Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated before any release that adds advertising, analytics, online services, or other data handling. The current version and effective date will remain available at this public URL.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact Classic Forest Games at classic.forest.games@gmail.com.
