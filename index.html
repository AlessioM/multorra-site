Do the math.
Grow the army.

Lead a squad of archers, mages, knights and rogues up a three-lane road. Every gate asks a quick question — 7 × 6, 63 ÷ 9, 48 + 17, or which word is spelled wrong — and the right swipe sends new heroes running to your side. Pick the easy gate for a few, or the hard one for many.

Every gate is a sum

  1. 1

    Run

    Your squad runs up the road on its own and fires at everything ahead. Swipe left or right to change lane and dodge orcs and skeletons.

  2. 2

    Solve

    Steer into a gate and time slows down. Four answers sit around a dial: swipe toward the right one (or tap it) before the rim runs out. One star is an easy question, three stars a hard one with a bigger reward.

  3. 3

    Grow

    Right answers recruit new heroes or trigger bombs and rapid fire. A wrong answer? The road speeds up for a few seconds — hang on and keep going.

More than flashcards

All four operations

Adding, taking away, times tables and division, from 3 + 4 up to 86 × 7 on the last levels. Pick Easy, Medium or Hard in the menu: Easy asks only additions up to 20, Medium adds up to 100 and asks the single-digit times tables.

Spelling, too

Switch on spelling in English, German or Italian: four words, one of them misspelled. Find it, and the game shows the right spelling. The difficulty sets the word length (up to 4 letters on Easy). Mix it with math or play words only.

Easy or hard, your call

Gates come in pairs: a quick one-star sum for a small reward, or a three-star challenge for a bigger squad.

Slow motion, not a buzzer

The game slows down while you think instead of stopping cold. A wrong answer only means a short rush — no lives lost for one slip.

Four kinds of hero

Archers fire piercing arrows, mages throw orbs that burst over a group, knights hurl spinning axes through a line of foes and rogues throw a stream of daggers. Mix them for the best squad.

A map to climb

40 levels in six lands: a green meadow, snowy peaks, high cliffs, a river of bridges, an iron prison and a rowdy tavern, each with a boss at the end.

No account, no ads

Just play. No sign-up, no ads and nothing collected — progress is saved on your device only.
